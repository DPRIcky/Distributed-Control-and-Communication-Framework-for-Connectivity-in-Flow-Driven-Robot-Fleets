# Reading List

These are the papers, books and theses collected while working on this research. The PDFs are **not** in this repository because they are copyrighted by their publishers. Look up each title to find the paper. My own notes are in [Notes.txt](Notes.txt).

- Decentralized Control of Connectivity for Multi-Agent Systems (M. C. De Gennaro & A. Jadbabaie)
- Distributed Connectivity Control of Mobile Networks
- Consensus-Based Distributed Connectivity Control in Multi-Agent Systems
- Decentralized Connectivity-Preserving Deployment of Large-Scale Robot Swarms
- Decentralized Reconfigurable Multi-Robot Coordination from Local Connectivity and Collision Avoidance Specifications

- A Distributed Method for Detecting Critical Edges and Increasing Edge Connectivity in Undirected Networks (Venkateswaran et al., CDC 2024)
- Hybrid Topology Control: A Dynamic Leader-Based Distributed Edge-Addition and Deletion Mechanism
- Deadlock Resolution of Connected Multi-Agent Systems using Hierarchical Control
- CBF-based connectivity (Elsevier), marked "to cite"
